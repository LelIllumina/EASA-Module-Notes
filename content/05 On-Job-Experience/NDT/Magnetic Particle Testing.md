## Overview

A method of NDT for checking External and some Internal cracks using Magnets. Only works on certain materials.

## Applicability

Requires the Part being tested to be Ferromagnetic

## Requirements

- For Cross-sectional cracks Longitudinal / Coil-shot Method is used.
- For Parallel cracks Circular magnetization is used.
- Depainting may be required

## Steps

1. Magnetize the part with the appropriate method.
2. Spray or submerge the part in ferrous particle fluid.
3. Observe for cracks in ample visible light, if using fluorescent particles use black light.
4. Demagnetize the part.

## Definitions

- Cracks:
	- Cross-sectional: Width wise cracks
	- Parallel: Length wise cracks
- Permeability: How easy a material can be magnetized
- Retentivity: How hard it is to demagnetized a material
- MPIU: Magnetic Particle Inspection Unit; The tool used to (de-)magnetize and check parts for faults
- Field Indicator: Used to detect the presence and measure the strength of a magnetic field
- Centrifugal Tube: used for concentration testing; Measures the amount of ferrous particles in fluid. Recommended concentration is 0.1–0.4 ml in 100ml
- Magnetic Flux: measure of total magnetic field in an area
