## Overview

A method of NDT for checking external cracks using visual identification. The commonest and usually first type of method used of NDT

## Applicability

This method is only applicable for external cracks. It cannot detect internal damage.

## Requirements

- Depainted Part
- Sufficient Quality of Eyesight
- Illumination of 100 fc (Foot candle)
- Visible Spectrum Light
- Magnifying Glass of 10X zoom

## Definitions

- Radiometer: An instrument used to measure the intensity of light
- Foot-Candle: The measurement of the amount of Lumen in and Area of 1 square foot. 100 fc is 100 lumen in 1 square feet.
