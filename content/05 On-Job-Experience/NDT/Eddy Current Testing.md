## Overview

A method of NDT for external and near-surface (open to surface and subsurface) cracks. Only works on certain materials

## Applicability

Requires the part being tested to be of Aluminum Alloys or Steel (Material needs to conduct electricity)

## Requirements

Depending on the Area to be tested different probes can be used:

- General Purpose / General Surface Probe
- Right Angle Probe
- Bolt Hole Probe

Furthermore, Probes can be either

- Shielded: Used at borders and edges to reduce boundary effect.
- Non-Shielded: General Purpose.

Teflon Tape is wrapped around the probe tip to help it last longer and protect from wear and tear.

## Steps

- Move the probe above the part.
- If a crack is under the probe it will cause a disturbance in the measured impedance
- This is due to the difference in the secondary field created by the eddy current causing a change in impedance in the primary circuit

## Definitions

- Eddy Current Probe: Also called a search unit. An instrument used to produce eddy currents.
- Impedance: Total resistance in a circuit
