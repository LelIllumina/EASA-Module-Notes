## Overview

NDT, which stands for Non-Destructive Testing is the process of checking Parts (of an aircraft) for flaws such as cracks. It is part of the broader process of "Overhauling"

## Key Points

Types of NDT consist of:

1. [[Visual Testing]]
2. [[Penetrant Testing]]
3. [[Magnetic Particle Testing]]
4. [[Eddy Current Testing]]

## NDT Back-up Procedure

Used to confirm that a part is in fact damaged, redundancy, in it you perform a:

- Change of Equipment
- Change of Staff
- Change of Method

## Definitions

- External Cracks: Cracks which are open to the surface.
- Internal Cracks: Cracks which underneath the surface.
