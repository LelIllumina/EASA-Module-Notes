## Key Points

- Mass is the quantity of matter
- Weight is the quantity of the gravitational force acting on a mass
	- As the distance of a mass increases from earth, earths gravitational force acting upon it decreases, reducing its weight
- Inertia is the tendency of an object to stay stationary when not acted upon, or stay in motion when not acted upon
