## Topics

- Fluid Dynamics

## Key Points

- Fluids include Gases and Liquids
- Usually liquids are incompressible while gasses can be compressed
- Liquids are usually more viscous than gasses
- A standard Temperature of 4C is used when measuring relative [[Density]]
	- This is because at 4C, Pure water has a density of 1000kg/m^3
	- Relative Density is determined on the basis of pure water at 4C
	- RD is a ratio, not a value with a unit
	- This is often referred to as a Specific Gravity
	- Air is used to measure the RD of gasses and water for liquids
	- Both have an RD of 1

## Definitions

- Viscosity: The resistance of a fluid/High viscosity means low flow
- $$ Density = \rho = \frac{m}{v} $$
- $$ \text{Relative Density} = \frac{\text{Density of Substance}}{\text{Density of Water}} $$
- 1013.25mb is Standard atmospheric pressure.
