## Topics

- Atmosphere

## Key Points

- Air density decreases as altitude increases;
- This is because air on the higher (atmosphere) layers weighs down on the lower layers compressing it
- Approximate safe limit for being in an aircraft without special equipment is ~10000m
- Temp falls at approx. 6.5C per 1000m (or 2C per 1000m)

## Definitions

- Mass of a cubic meter of Air at ground level: ~1.225 kg
- Mass of a cubic meter of Water at ground level: 1000 kg
- Viscosity is the tendency of one layer of a fluid to move with another, which determines its rate of flow
