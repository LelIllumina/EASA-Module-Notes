## Key Points

- Flight phases
	- Takeoff
	- Climb
	- Cruise
	- Descent
	- Landing
- Aircraft (plane) sections
	- Nose
	- Fuselage
	- Wings
	- Empennage
- Aerodynamics should focus on reducing Drag
- Weight reduces in flight as fuel is used and exhausted
- Dihedral wings have their **wingtips** above their **wing roots**
- The angle of incidence is the angle between the Longitudinal Axis and the (extension of the) chord line
- This differs from the Angle of attack which is the angle between the (extension of the) chord line and the vector of Relative Airflow

## Definitions

- MTOW: Maximum Takeoff Weight
- Relative Airflow: The direction of the air hitting the aircraft
- Longitudinal Axis: Line running through the center of the aircraft/fuselage, lengthwise (nose to Empennage)
- Chord Line: Line running from the leading edge to the trailing age
