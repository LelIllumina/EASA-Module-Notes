## Key Points

- Angles:
	- Flat wings are Neutral
	- Anhedral (down tilted) are unstable
	- Dihedral (up tilted) are stable
- Mounting Position
	- High Mounted is stable
	- Mid mounted is neutral
	- low mounted is unstable
- Fighter planes usually want "instability" for quicker rolling and turning
	- As such they either have neutral mid mounted wings or unstable low mounted wings
	- And usually Anhedral wings
