## Key Points

- Aerodynamics is the study of the movement of air
- Chapters are:
	1. Physics of Atmosphere: Properties and divisions of the air.
	2. Basic Aerodynamics: How an aircraft should be built.
	3. Flight Phases: Interactions and Pros/Cons of air and surfaces.
	4. Speed: Differences between higher and lower speeds.
	5. Control System/Surfaces: How they work and why.
