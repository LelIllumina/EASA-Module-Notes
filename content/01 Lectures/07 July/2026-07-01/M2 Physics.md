## Topics

- Physical Nature of Matter

## Key Points

 - Matter is made of molecules; the smallest unit of a substance
 - All molecules of a singular type are identical physically and chemically.
 - Atoms are constantly in motion (Brownian motion), the amount of motion determines the matters state (solid, liquid, gas)
 - The amount of motion is determined by the amount of heat energy
 - Physical state does not affect chemical properties
 - Solids are tightly packed because their molecules have strong bonds holding them tightly together
 - Solid to Liquid transition requires heat, as the heat energy causes the molecules to increase in motion causing the atoms to be farther apart.
 - Solids to liquid does to change volume (significantly)
 - Liquids do not have a rigid shape but have partial bonds which causes surface tension
 - Surface tension prevents liquids from expanding
 - Liquid to gas transition requires heat, enough to break the bonds of surface tension and convert the liquid into a gas.
 - Lower Pressure causes lower Boiling Points
 - Lower Boiling point is directly proportional to Lower Pressure
 - Pressure is inversely proportional to Altitude.
 - Gases can be compressed unlike solids and liquids.
 - At regular atmospheric pressure, Water freezes at 0C, liquid between 1 and 99C, and boils at 100C, At lower Pressure the boiling point lowers to <100C
 - 

## Definitions

- Solid: Shape and volume are definite, incompressible, Tightly packed molecules.
 - Liquid: Shape can change, but volume cannot, incompressible, Not as tightly packed molecules.
 - Gas: neither shape nor volume definite, can be compressed, Molecules are not tightly packed.

## Examples

- Solid: Ice
- Liquid: Water
- Gas: Steam
