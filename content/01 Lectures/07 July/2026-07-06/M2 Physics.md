## Topics

- Forces

## Key Points

- The resultant is the sum of 2 or more forces to complete a triangle/polygon of forces
- The Equilibrant is a force equal in length but opposite in direction to the Net force, which means adding it would cancel both forces resulting in 0 Net force
- Drawing Vectors follows the head to tail rule where each additional force's tail starts from the previous forces head
- Planes are 2 dimensional shapes defining a "view"
- A couple force is a physical system of two equal parallel forces
- Coplanar forces Laws:
	1. The line of action of the resultant of any two coplanar forces must go through the intersecting point of the 2 forces' Line of action
	2. If any number of coplanar forces on a plane are not in equilibrium, they can be reduced to a resultant force and a couple forces
	3. If three coplanar forces are in equilibrium, their line of action must be concurrent, i.e. they intersect at one point

## Definitions

- Plane: A 2 dimensional surface extending infinitely in all directions  
- Coplanar forces exist on the same plane
