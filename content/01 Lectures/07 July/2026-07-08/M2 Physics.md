## Topics

- Revision
- Concurrent forces

## Key Points

- Vector addition is commutative (order of addition does not matter)
- A body cannot change acceleration or direction without a Net force > 0
- However, an applied force can also causes changes in shape and size through
	- Tension: Pulling force
	- Compression: Pushing force
	- Torsion / Shear: Twisting force
- A single force can be divided into to its 2 components (Vertical and Horizontal)

## Definitions

- Concurrent forces are forces acting from the same point

## Examples

- Force = mass\*acceleration (F= ma)
