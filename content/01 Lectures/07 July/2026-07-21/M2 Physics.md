## Topics

- Stress

## Key Points

- Types of stress include
	- Tension
	- Compression
	- Torsion
	- Bending
	- Shear

## Definitions

- Tension: Pull (outwards) force
- Compression: Push (inwards) force
- Torsion: Twisting force (both tension and compression at play)
- Bending: Straight to Curved
- Shear: Slicing/tearing force

## Examples

- Tension: Tug of war
- Compression: rivets Themselves
- Torsion: Twisting in a crankshaft
- Bending: Cantilever end, wing tips
- Shear: The sheets being riveted
