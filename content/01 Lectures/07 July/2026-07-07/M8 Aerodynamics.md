## Key Points

- The atmosphere consists of different gases;
	- 78% Nitrogen
	- 21% Oxygen
	- 0.9% Argon
	- 0.1% trace gases
- The 2 relevant layers of the atmosphere are:
	- The Troposphere (lower)
	- The Stratosphere (above)
- The different layers have different:
	- Pressure: Lower altitude has higher Air pressure
	- Density: Lower altitude has denser air
- Mass of 1 cubic meter of
	- Water is 1000kg
	- Air is 1.204kg

## Definitions

- Pressure is Force over Area
- Density is mass over volume

$$
\begin{aligned}
Pressure = P = \frac{F}{A} 
\\ \\
Density = \rho = \frac{m}{v}
\end{aligned}
$$
