## Topics

## Key Points

- Stress, strain, elasticity
- Stress is inversely proportional to Area
- Stress types:
	- Tensile stress: being pulled and/or stretched
	- Compression stress: being pushed and/or squeezed
	- Torsion stress: being twisted
- Stain occurs when a material experiences more stress than a certain limit(Which depends on the material)

## Definitions

- Stress: Force/Area; Force on a structures area;
