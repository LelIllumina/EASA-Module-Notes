This folder contains notes quickly jotted down during in person classes. They have not been cross checked with books or other sources so may not be fully accurate. They are also somewhat unordered and messy.

Each entry contains notes for one period only, for Modules that have multiple classes in a day each period has its own file. A single note contains every topic and concept discussed in that period, for revision notes broken down by Chapter or concept check out [[02 Modules/Index|Modules]].
