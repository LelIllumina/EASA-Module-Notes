## Key Points

- High Lift devices:
	- Flaps
	- Slats
- Fuel is held in the Wings
- During emergency landings, over limit fuel is jettisoned to reduce weight on wings and allow safe landing without damage to wings.
- Drag increases proportionally to speed, and crossing supersonic speeds causes drag to increase at an even higher rate
- The troposphere contains water vapor which causes clouds
- 

## Definitions

- Layers:
	- Troposphere: Lowest level, 7500m at poles and 20000m at the equator.
	- Tropopause: Between troposphere and stratosphere, Temperature at -56.5C
	- Stratosphere: Above Tropopause, No water vapor
- 1013.25 millibars or 29.92 inches ( or 760mm) of mercury on a barometer is the average air pressure at sea level
