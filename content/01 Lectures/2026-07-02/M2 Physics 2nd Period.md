## Key Points

- Molecules are matter, a molecule is the smallest part of a substance
- Physical states of a substance do not affect its chemical properties
- Solids have a fixed/zero degree of motion, they do not conform to their container and are incompressible
- Liquids have more degree of motion and its molecules are farther apart but they are still incompressible, but they do conform to their container
- Surface tension occurs because molecules in a liquid are still partially bonded, this causes the liquid to not expand in all directions (indefinitely)
- Gases' molecules are relatively very far apart allowing them to be compressible, they conform to their container

- Understanding forces helps an engineer understand the relation between lift, drag, thrust, weight, and other forces.
- Understanding different types of equilibrium
- Scalars are quantities with intrinsic amounts but no direction, they can be represented on a 1 dimensional scale/graph
- Vectors have an amount and a direction for that amount, they're usually represented with a line and arrowhead for the direction.
- The sum of forces on an object is its resultant force.
