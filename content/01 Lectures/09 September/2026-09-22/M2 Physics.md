## Topics

- Engine cycle

## Key Points

- An engine cycle has 4 repeating steps:
	- Intake: of air and fuel
	- Compression: using a piston
	- Combustion: pushing back the engine
	- Exhaust: of smoke from combustion
- An Engine has 6 parts, from cold section to hot section:
	1. Air inlet
	2. Compressor
	3. Diffuser
	4. Combustion Chamber
	5. Turbine
	6. Exhaust
- Compressors have moving parts and add pressure, [[Temperature]] ,and energy
- Diffusers have no moving parts and slow down the fast compressed air to increase its static pressure
