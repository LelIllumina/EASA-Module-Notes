## Key Points

- Both fire and Combustion
	- Oxygen
	- Fuel
	- Heat
- The resulting chain Chemical Reaction produces fire
- Combustion is used in 2 types of engines: Internal and External Combustion Engine
- Internal Combustion Engines have heat energy utilized inside the engine itself and include
	- Piston [[Engines]]
	- Gas Turbine [[Engines]]
- External combustion e ngines have heat energy moved from one place to another to produce kinetic energy, For e g steam engines

---

- The 4 stroke Otto cycle describes the method in which a piston produces
- The strokes in order are:
	- Intake Stroke: Inlet of Air + Fuel Mixture while Piston moves from TDC to BDC
	- Compression Stroke: Piston moves from BDC to TDC compressing the mixture
	- Power Stroke: Spark plug ignites mixture and combusts; Piston moves from TDC back to BDC from combustion's increase in pressure. This provides momentum to the crankshaft
	- Exhaust Stroke: Opens exhaust valve to let combusted fuel gasses escape

## Definitions

- TDC: Top Dead Center
- BDC: Bottom Dead Center
