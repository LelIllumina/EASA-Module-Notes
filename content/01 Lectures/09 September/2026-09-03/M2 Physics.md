## Topics

- Specific Heat and Heat Capacity

## Key Points

- Heat capacity depends on the mass and specific heat capacity of a body.
- A denser object usually has higher heat capacity

## Definitions

- Heat Capacity is the amount of heat energy required to raise an objects temperature by 1 degree.
- Specific Heat Capacity is the amount of energy required to raise 1 kg of a substance by 1 kelvin.

  $$ \text{Specific Heat Capacity} = \frac{J}{{Kg}\times{K}} $$
