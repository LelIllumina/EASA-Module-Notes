## Topics

- Chemical Nature of Matter

## Key Points

- Atoms
	- Atoms are made of electrons, protons, neutrons, which determine their properties
	- Protons are positively charged, Neutrons are neutral and electrons are negatively charged
	- Electrons exist in the Electron shells of an atom, the last/farthest out shell is the valence shell
	- The center of the atom is the nucleus which consists of protons and neutrons
	- First shell has 2 electrons, 2nd shell has 8 electrons and so on with 2n^2 where n is the shell number.
	- An atom is neutrally charged if the # of protons is equal to the number of Electrons
- Periodic Table
	- Arranged in terms of increasing atomic number
	- Columns are groups, with elements sharing properties
	- Rows are Periods, same number of Electron shells
- Molecules are bonds of multiple atoms
- Single Atom molecules exist because their valence shell is already filled
- H20 has an Oxygen Atom with 2 Hydrogen atoms bonded to it on either side
	- Hydrogen is a flammable gas
	- Oxygen is also a gas
	- Water is a nonflammable liquid
- Valence shell
	- A full outer shell means the atom is chemically stable
	- a complete valence shell also mean the material itself is a good insulator because the electrons are bound to the nucleus
	- Conductors have an incomplete outer shell making them more chemically active

## Definitions

- Nucleons are the sum of Protons and Neutrons
- Atomic Number is the number of protons only in the atom
- Atomic mass is the sum of the neutrons and protons (Nucleons)
- A covalent bond is when 2 or more atoms share an Electron
- Insulators are chemically stable and have higher resistance to electric flow
- Conductors are good conductors of electricity as they have valence electrons.
- Compounds are elements bonded together
- Mixtures are elements physically together but not bonded
