## Overview

Fluid Dynamics are the study of a flowing gasses or liquids (fluids). Their properties and how they change with temperature, density, viscosity etc.

## Key Points

- [[Density]] and the resulting relative density are measurements used in aviation for fluids like fuel.
	- [[Buoyancy]] Is related to RD and describes if an object floats in a fluid.
- [[Viscosity]], the resistance to flow is observed in similar fluids.
- Streamline flow (or Laminar flow) is smooth steady flow of a fluid
- Turbulent flow (which can be caused by large irregularities) is flowing fluid which has eddies and vortex
- [[Venturi Tube]] Demonstrates Bernoulli's Principle
