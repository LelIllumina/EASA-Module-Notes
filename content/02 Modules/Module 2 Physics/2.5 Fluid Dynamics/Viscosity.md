## Overview

[[Viscosity]] is a fluids resistance to flow (To change in shape and/or to move)

## Key Points

- Fluids with higher internal friction have higher viscosity
	- The layers inside of a fluid rub against each other and restrict flow.
	- Higher restriction means more friction, thus higher viscosity.
- Even low viscosity substances have *Some* viscosity, as seen by stirring a cup of water and all the liquid being stirred together
- Viscosity decreases as a substances temperature increases

## Definitions

## Examples

- Honey is "thick and sticky" and has high viscosity (Viscous)
- Water flows easily and has low viscosity (Mobile)
