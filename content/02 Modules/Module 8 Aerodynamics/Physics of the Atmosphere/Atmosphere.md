## Overview

Atmosphere refers to the bubble of air surrounding the earth at a depth of ~800km (500 miles). It contains multiple types of gasses and has different properties at different altitudes.

## Key Points

- The atmosphere consists of different gases;
	- 78% Nitrogen
	- 21% Oxygen
	- 0.9% Argon
	- 0.1% trace gases
		- Carbon Dioxide
		- Neon
		- Helium
		- Methane
		- Nitrous Oxide
		- Ozone
		- Krypton, etc.
- The 2 relevant layers of the atmosphere are:
	- The Troposphere
		- Lower level of the atmosphere
		- From the surface to the tropopause
		- Its depth varies between 7500m (~28000 feet) at the poles and 20000m (~54000 feet) at the equator
		- Contains clouds caused by water vapor
		- Vertical air currents
		- Reason for most weather conditions
	- The Stratosphere (above)
	0	- Above the tropopause
		- Extremely thin air
		- No water vapor; no weather
	- There also exists the Tropopause which is between both layers and has a constant temperature of -56.5C
- The different layers have different [[Pressure]], [[Density]], [[Temperature|Temperature]], [[Air Viscosity]], And [[Humidity]].
