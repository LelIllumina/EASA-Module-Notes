## Overview

Air temperature falls at a predictable rate with increasing altitude within the troposphere, then holds constant through the tropopause.

## Key Points

- Lower altitudes have higher temperatures
- This is because the solid earth absorbs heat from the sun and spreads it to the nearby air
- Temperature falls at 6.5C per 1000m (2C per 1000 feet) — this is the standard lapse rate
- The Tropopause has a constant temperature of -56.5C

## Definitions

- Lapse Rate: the rate at which atmospheric temperature decreases with an increase in altitude
