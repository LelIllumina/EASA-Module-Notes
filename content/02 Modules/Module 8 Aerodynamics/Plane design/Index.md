## Overview

This section focuses on the effects of different design choices when making an aircraft.

## Key Points

- There are multiple types of [[Engines]] depending on the aircraft and producer's needs.
- Different aspects of the [[Wings]] affect lift and stability.
- Different sections of the aircraft include the
	- Nose
	- Fuselage
	- [[Wings]]
	- Empennage

## Definitions

- Lateral/Longitudinal: See [[#Images]]
- Longitudinal Axis: Imaginary Line running through the center of the aircraft/fuselage, lengthwise (nose to Empennage)
- Starboard: the right side of the ship when facing the forward (same direction as the nose)
- Port: as Starboard, but for the left side
