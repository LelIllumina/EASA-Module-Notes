## Overview

Air Viscosity describes how air resists internal movement, similar to friction between solids.

## Key Points

- It is the tendency of one layer of air to move with the layer next to it (like friction in solids)

## Definitions

- Viscosity: the tendency of one layer of a fluid to move with another, which determines its rate of flow
